📊 Calculated Results

📐 Formulas Used

Feed Rate (in/min) = RPM × Number of Flutes × Chipload
Plunge Rate (in/min) = Feed Rate × Plunge Factor
Stepover Distance (in) = Tool Diameter × (Stepover % / 100)
Ramp Distance (in) = Depth of Cut / tan(Ramp Angle)
Material Removal Rate (in³/min) = Feed Rate × Depth of Cut × Stepover Distance

Understanding Depth of Cut (DOC)

DOC = Tool Dia × Material Factor × Operation Factor × Bit Type Factor

Calculator determines safe DOC and splits your total depth into multiple passes. Example: 0.060" total ÷ 0.015" per pass = 4 passes.

⚠️ Multiple shallow passes are safer than one deep cut. Always test in scrap first.
